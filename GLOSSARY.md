# FinScope

FinScope brings a person's financial records together so they can understand their financial position.

## Language

**Financial account** — A record in FinScope representing an account a person tracks, such as a bank account or a credit card account.

**Opening balance** — The amount held or owed in a financial account when tracking begins. It establishes a starting financial position and contributes nothing to the period's income or expenses.

**Current cash balance** — The cash held across tracked bank and brokerage accounts after all recorded activity.

**Reporting period** — The inclusive date range used to report everyday income, expenses and net cashflow on the Overview.

**Realised profit or loss** — The difference between the proceeds of a sale and the cost attributed to the units sold.

**Unrealised profit or loss** — The difference between the current value of units still held and the cost attributed to those units.

**Execution price** — The price per unit at which a purchase or sale took place, before fees.

**Average cost per unit** — The remaining cost of a holding, including attributable buy fees, divided by its remaining units. Each brokerage account has its own average for each security.

**Market price** — A dated price quote for a security used to value units held.
